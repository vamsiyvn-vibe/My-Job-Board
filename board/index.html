Import LinkedIn Connections

How to get your Connections CSV from LinkedIn:

  1. Click your profile picture in the top right of LinkedIn and select Settings & Privacy.
  2. Go to Data privacy in the left sidebar.
  3. Under the How LinkedIn uses your data section, click on Get a copy of your data.
  4. Select the second option: Want something in particular? Select the data files you're most interested in.
  5. Check the Connections box and click Request archive.
  6. Enter your password. Within ~10 minutes, you will receive an email containing a download link for a zip archive containing Connections.csv.
